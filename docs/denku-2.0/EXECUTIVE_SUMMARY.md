# DENKU 2.0 — Executive Summary

> Research package: `docs/denku-2.0/01–20`. Investigated 2026-08-25: full forensic audit of
> Creato (rendered pages, DOM/styles, network, 6MB bundle mining, subdomains, app-store
> listings), Denku repo current state, US competitor & pricing research (sourced), API cost
> research. **No production code was modified.**

## 1. What Creato does exceptionally well
Business architecture, not technology: an ecosystem (agency + platform + vertical SaaS +
community + marketplace apps) where every surface cross-sells; productized offers (named plans,
deliverable counts, revision counts, delivery days); "show don't claim" marketing (animated
product mock-UIs, embed code on the page); a vertical self-serve wedge (Ikasagent on ikas,
₺6.7k–22.5k/mo, 250+ stores) riding borrowed distribution; an internal agency OS (proposals →
payments → subscriptions → credits → partners); multi-provider LLM tiers with cost pass-through.
Meanwhile its celebrated visuals are an off-the-shelf ThemeForest theme (AiHub), its voice
product has no public proof, and nothing about it can serve the US market.

## 2. What Denku currently lacks
Not product depth — Denku's platform (voice pipeline with a never-dead-end artifact guarantee,
channel-agnostic conversations, CRM memory, versioned employee manifests, honest billing
enforcement, the new Home/Inbox/CRM/AI Team IA) exceeds the benchmark and the US SMB field.
Denku lacks: **a launched product (still dark behind flags), any acquisition machine, packaging
(templates/ladder/packs), experiential proof, SMS & real calendar booking, and a front door
worthy of the product.** Plus live legal exposure: SOC2/HIPAA claims on the site.

## 3. What Denku should become
**The AI employee platform for US service businesses.** "Hire your first AI employee. It answers
every call. It never forgets a customer." Self-serve SaaS, printed prices, month-to-month,
honesty as brand law. The unclaimed intersection: receptionist tools have no memory; platform
suites (Podium $500–800 real spend, contracts) treat AI as an add-on; AI-employee brands don't
do inbound SMB comms. Denku's built-but-dark product already sits exactly there.

## 4. Top 10 opportunities (doc 19 matrix)
1. Deploy & flip everything already built (days; the standing P0)
2. "Talk to Denku" public live demo line + web call (the wow no competitor has)
3. Website 2.0: original visual system + real-UI storytelling + honest pricing ladder
4. SMS + missed-call-text-back (the #1 US SMB feature; pure adapter work on existing pipeline)
5. Real calendar booking (R-020 — the feature buyers verify first)
6. Instant automated AI Audit as lead magnet (Creato's front door, automated + free)
7. 5 named employee templates + hire-style onboarding with audit prefill
8. Minute/message packs + explainable billing (trust as ARPU)
9. Weekly outcome digest ("what your employee did this week")
10. Web-chat widget + SEO/AEO industry-page engine + marketplace listings

## 5. Top 10 things NOT to build
1. Custom-software/RPA agency arm (Creato's headcount business)
2. AI Studio as a product line pre-revenue (crowded: AdCreative/Creatify/Icon; gate: ≥20 asks)
3. Outbound sales/cold-calling AI (TCPA; Artisan/11x turf)
4. WhatsApp/Telegram for US v1
5. Enterprise custom agents (Sierra/Decagon ACV game)
6. A dashboard rebuild (Phase-2 IA is right; re-skin + gaps only)
7. Proprietary voice infrastructure (stay HYBRID on Vapi, abstracted)
8. A community program; 9. Review-management/reputation features (Podium's turf)
10. Any feature that ships dark ("inert until migrated" is banned for customer-facing work)

## 6. Recommended business model
Self-serve SaaS: Solo $149 / Team $399 ★ / Scale $899 (employees × channels × minutes), packs as
top-ups, optional "We'll set it up" SKU, annual −15–20%, hard caps + pause (marketed as a
feature), enterprise quiet-quote only. Phase 3: partner/white-label program (validated by Creato
+ GHL's ecosystem). North-star: weekly booked-or-resolved outcomes per customer.

## 7. Recommended product architecture
Keep the two-plane model and Phase-2 IA (Home / Inbox / CRM / AI Team / Analytics / Billing /
Settings). Objects: Employee (versioned) · Channel · Conversation · Contact · Artifact(+Booking)
· Outcome. Channels: Voice → SMS → Web chat → IG (Meta-gated) → Email. Five named templates.

## 8. Recommended website architecture
Small surface, deep funnel: home · 5 employee pages · 4 industry pages · pricing · demo · audit
· 3 product pages · trust. Homepage: hero → sourced-stat proof strip → live demo → how hiring
works → templates → real-UI workday scroll story → memory → pricing → audit → honest FAQ.

## 9. Recommended visual direction
**"A warm employee in a dark suit"** — teal-black canvas (#0A1414), bone ink (#F7F5F1), copper
signature (#C89468), Fraunces display + Inter, bone-glass cards. Signatures: the Employee Card,
the Thread motif, real-UI frames with labeled sample data. Explicitly avoids the category's
navy+violet+Syne uniform (which is literally a purchasable theme). GSAP ambient motion,
reduced-motion mandatory. Retire the Spline robot.

## 10. Highest-priority first 5 sprints (doc 20)
**D0 Turn It On** (deploy, flip flags, merge Sprint 9–14 branch, kill false compliance claims) →
**D1 Talk to Denku** (public demo line, dogfooded) → **D2 Offer & Billing Honesty** (ladder,
packs, explainable invoice) → **D3 Templates & Hiring Onboarding** (5 templates, name-your-
employee, test-call in flow; then *actively try to sell* — Gate A) → **D4 Website 2.0** (in
parallel where safe) / **D5 SMS & Missed-Call Rescue**.

---

## WHAT WE SHOULD BUILD FIRST

**Nothing — first we ship what is already built (D0), and make it publicly experienceable (D1).**
The first *construction* is D2+D3 (offer + templates), because they convert the existing
platform into something a US buyer can understand and purchase in ten minutes. The first *new
capability* is SMS missed-call rescue (D5) — the single highest-demand, lowest-effort,
US-specific feature, and the proof that the channel architecture five sprints paid for was worth
it. Creato is the benchmark for the business machine around a product; on the product itself,
Denku is already ahead — the entire 2.0 program exists to stop hiding that.

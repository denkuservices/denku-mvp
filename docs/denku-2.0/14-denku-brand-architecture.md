# 14 — Denku Brand Architecture

## 1. Masterbrand decision

**One brand: Denku.** (Never "Denku AI" — existing rule stands.) No sub-brands, no ecosystem
naming at launch. Creato fragments into CREATO Agency / CREATO App / Ikasagent / Trendio because
it serves two different businesses (services + SaaS); Denku is one business and should spend its
tiny awareness budget on one name.

- Product surfaces are **descriptors, not brands**: "Denku — AI Employees", "the Denku Inbox",
  "Denku CRM". Employee templates get *role names* ("Receptionist", "Missed-Call Rescuer") —
  never trademark-style names that compete with the masterbrand.
- The one permitted flourish: customers **name their own AI employee** during onboarding (the
  hire metaphor made personal). The default suggested name is "Denku" — turning the brand into
  the employee ("Denku answered 47 calls for you this week").

## 2. Brand idea

> **"Hire your first AI employee."**

- Category term: **AI Employee** (US-validated by Podium/GHL adoption; Denku's existing rule —
  customer-facing "AI", never "agent" — extends to: never "bot", never "assistant" in headlines).
- Tagline system: functional headline + outcome subline.
  - Primary: **"Hire your first AI employee. It answers every call."**
  - Vertical variants: "…It books your appointments." / "…It texts back every missed call."
- The memory hook (differentiator line): **"It never forgets a customer."**

## 3. Voice & tone

| Trait | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Plain-spoken operator | "Answers your phone 24/7. Books the job." | "Revolutionize your communication paradigm" |
| Honest to a fault | print prices, name limits ("Instagram: receiving only, for now"), show the rounding rule | "starting from…", hidden fees, fake counters |
| Calm confidence | let live demos and real numbers talk | hype adjectives ("cutting-edge", "game-changing") |
| Employer's language | hire, shift, handoff, workload, day-one | prompts, models, tokens, LLM (reserve for a later dev page) |

The honesty rule is brand law, not compliance: it produced the product's best features (truthful
counts, pause-at-cap) and it is the sharpest contrast against both Creato's unverifiable claims
and Podium's fee resentment.

## 4. Naming the offer ladder (doc 04/19 alignment)

- Plans: **Solo / Team / Scale** (replacing starter/growth/scale — "an employee, then a team").
- Top-ups: **Minute Packs** and **Message Packs** (literal names; no credit abstractions).
- Setup SKU: **"We'll set it up"** (done-for-you onboarding).
- Lead magnet: **Denku AI Audit** — "See what your phone is costing you."
- Demo: **"Talk to Denku"** — one phone number + web-call button, everywhere.

## 5. Trust architecture (zero-customer proof stack)

Until logos exist, proof is *experiential and quantified*:
1. Live demo line answered by Denku itself (the self-agent, à la Creato — but public).
2. Instant AI Audit with sourced benchmarks (62% missed-call stat etc., always cited).
3. A **Trust page** that is honest about status: security practices, RLS/data isolation,
   spend caps, data retention, "what we don't claim" (no SOC 2 *yet* — remove the false claims;
   publish the roadmap to it). In the US, a candid trust page from a small vendor outperforms
   inflated badges.
4. Founder-visible support ("text us; a human answers — or our AI does, and says so").

## 6. Brand ↔ product consistency rules

1. Every surface says **AI Employee** (finishes F-006's terminology sweep; the word "agent"
   survives only in Settings→Advanced).
2. Every number shown in marketing must be reproducible in the product (extends R-018 honesty to
   the website; kills fake dashboards Creato-style — Denku renders real UI with labeled sample
   data).
3. One design language across marketing + auth + onboarding + dashboard (doc 17) — the four-
   dialect era ends; brand IS the continuity from ad click to daily use.
4. The employee metaphor governs UX copy end-to-end: onboarding = "interview & hire" (already
   re-narrated on the branch), settings = "training", takeover = "stepping in", digest =
   "weekly report from your employee".

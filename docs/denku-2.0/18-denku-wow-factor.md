# 18 — Denku Wow Layer

> Creato's per-page "this company is serious" elements, then Denku's own wow stack — each scored
> for stop-scrolling power vs build cost. Rule: Denku's wow must be *true* (house honesty law),
> which is precisely what makes it unmatchable by theme-buyers.

## 1. What creates "serious" on Creato's pages (audit)

| Page | Element | Why it lands |
|---|---|---|
| agency home | typewriter hero + dark continuity + glass | instant genre signal ("this is an AI company") |
| agency home | live embed-code block (Prism) | "the product is real code you can install" |
| ai-agents | animated Agent Control Center mock (1.8K convs, 0.8s response, live bars) | shows an operations cockpit, implies maturity |
| ai-studio | Try-On/video catalog marquees + $-priced packs | breadth + buyability |
| ikasagent | auto-playing inbox with human-takeover flow; pipeline with AI scores; widget configurator | the product appears to run on the page |
| client portal login | "unified customer memory" rotating pitch with a concrete customer example (₺5,420 VIP, abandoned cart) | sells while you log in |
| corporate | AI Audit 98% gauge; "Build Successful" code theater | competence theater for executives |

Pattern: **every wow is a simulation of the product working.** None of it is verifiable. That is
the opening.

## 2. The Denku wow stack (prioritized)

### W1 — "Talk to Denku" live demo line ★ the centerpiece (P0, days of work)
A real US number + web-call button in the homepage hero, answered by a Denku AI employee
running on the actual production pipeline. As you speak, the hero renders the conversation in a
**real Inbox thread** — and when you ask for an appointment, a **real booking-request artifact
card** appears. End screen: "That call became this. Imagine it was your business." 
Why it wins: no SMB competitor lets you *experience* the product pre-signup; it proves latency,
voice quality, and the artifact guarantee in 60 seconds; every demo call is also QA + a lead in
Denku's own CRM (self-agent, dogfooded). Creato cannot copy this honestly — their voice product
has no public demo.

### W2 — Instant AI Audit (P1, ~3–4 wks)
Enter website + business phone → animated readiness report: missed-call exposure (sourced
benchmarks), response grade, channel map, $ recovery estimate, recommended employee template →
"Hire this employee" CTA that pre-fills onboarding with the audit's findings. Shareable report
URL. The Creato audit made instant, free, and self-serve — their EN front door, automated.

### W3 — The live truth counter (P1, days)
"Denku answered N calls this week · booked M appointments · rescued K missed calls" — computed
from Denku's own demo line + (later) opted-in aggregate customer stats, footnoted with
methodology. A zero-customer company showing *real* numbers beats fake logo bars; it grows into
the strongest section of the site as customers accumulate.

### W4 — The Workday scroll story (P1, with site build)
Three pinned scenes of the REAL product (labeled sample data): missed call at 6:04pm → Denku
answers → ticket + SMS follow-up → contact timeline grows → Monday outcome digest. The Thread
motif (doc 17) draws the connecting line. Creato fakes idealized UIs; Denku screenshots its own.

### W5 — Employee Card materialization (P1, with site build)
The doc-17 signature: hero badge assembles from drifting conversation fragments ("Can you fit us
in Thursday?" · "Your order shipped" · missed-call icon) → snaps into the named employee card
with "On shift" pulse. Repeated at onboarding finale as *your* employee's badge — marketing
motif becomes product moment.

### W6 — The honest pricing block (P1, hours)
A pricing-page section that *shows the math*: a 20-second call rounding to 1 minute, the cap
pausing service before overage, cancel-anytime. Styled as a feature, headlined "No surprise
invoices. Ever." Wow through candor — directly targets Podium-contract resentment (doc 11).

### W7 — Weekly outcome digest as public artifact (P2)
A beautiful shareable "what my AI employee did this week" report (opt-in) — retention feature
that doubles as organic acquisition (the Creato client-portal pitch, turned outward).

## 3. Explicitly rejected wow candidates

- 3D robot/Spline scenes (brand contradiction, gimmick decay) — retire the current one.
- Fake dashboards with invented metrics (violates house law; Creato's move, not ours).
- ROI calculators with fantasy sliders — the Audit replaces them with sourced estimates.
- Particle/starfield backgrounds; "neural network" visualizations; code-typing theater (wrong
  audience).

## 4. Sequencing

W1 (with Sprint 0 deploy) → W6 (pricing build) → W2+W3 (audit sprint) → W4+W5 (site build
sprint) → W7 (post-first-customers). Each is independently shippable; none blocks launch.
